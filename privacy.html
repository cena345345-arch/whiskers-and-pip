Whiskers and Pip - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Whiskers and Pip application collects and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive: your TikTok open ID, display name, and an access token that lets the App post videos to your account on your instruction. We do not collect your password.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the App: authenticating you and publishing the videos you choose to your own TikTok account. Nothing else.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except TikTok itself as required for the posting features to work.

4. Data storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the posting features you authorized. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

5. Children

The App is operated by an adult creator account. The cartoon content posted is family-friendly.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through TikTok direct message on the @whiskersandpip account.
