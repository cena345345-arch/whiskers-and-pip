Whiskers and Pip

Whiskers & Pip is a fun cartoon series about Whiskers, a curious orange cat, and Pip, a clever little grey mouse.

About this app

The Whiskers and Pip developer app connects the creator's TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and publishes the creator's own cartoon shorts to TikTok using the Content Posting API. Every post is published only on the creator's instruction to the creator's own account.

Features

Login with TikTok, upload cartoon videos, and publish them as TikTok posts with titles and hashtags.
